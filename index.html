Privacy first

Your business records stay on your phone.

Patel's Register is designed as an offline register. According to the operator manual, data stays on the device rather than being sent to external servers.

  • PIN and biometric authentication
  • Employee and salary records stored locally
  • Encrypted backup and restore workflow
  • No online account required for the core register
